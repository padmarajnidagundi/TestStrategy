Lean Canvas — TestStrategy Dashboard

One-page business model for the AI-powered test strategy dashboard initiative.

Problem

  • Testing efforts are fragmented across teams and tools.
  • Quality risks are identified late in release cycles.
  • Test strategy decisions lack shared, visible rationale.

Solution

  • Central dashboard for strategy, coverage, and risk insights.
  • AI-assisted recommendations for test prioritization.
  • Standardized templates for strategy planning and reporting.

Unique Value Proposition

  • Convert test planning into actionable, measurable outcomes.
  • Unify quality signals into one trusted decision surface.

Unfair Advantage

  • Domain-tested strategy framework aligned to enterprise QA.
  • Reusable dashboard artifacts and implementation playbooks.
  • Institutional knowledge captured in structured templates.

Channels

  • GitHub Pages public showcase.
  • Internal quality engineering forums and communities.
  • Leadership demos and release readiness reviews.

Customer Segments

  • QA leads and test architects.
  • Engineering managers and release managers.
  • Product teams requiring risk-based quality visibility.

Key Metrics

  • Reduction in escaped defects.
  • Increase in risk-based test coverage.
  • Cycle time from strategy definition to execution.

Cost Structure

  • Dashboard development and maintenance.
  • Data integration and environment setup.
  • Training and ongoing adoption support.

Revenue Streams / Impact

  • Lower quality-related rework costs.
  • Faster releases through focused test investment.
  • Productivity gains from reusable strategy assets.
